# OSINT Tools

## Free

#### Maltego
Graphical link analysis tool. Transforms for KRS, social media, domains, emails, phones. Community edition free, paid from $400/month. Best for relationship mapping.
https://www.maltego.com

---

#### SpiderFoot
Automated OSINT scanner — 200+ modules. DNS, email, domains, leaks, social media, dark web via API. Open source, free self-hosted.
https://github.com/smicallef/spiderfoot

---

#### Recon-ng
Framework OSINT in Metasploit style. Modules for domains, contacts, geolocation, social media. Free, CLI-based.
https://github.com/lanmaster53/recon-ng

---

#### theHarvester
Email, subdomain, IP, name collection from public sources. Google, Bing, Shodan, Hunter integration. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/laramies/theHarvester

---

#### OSINT Framework
Interactive map of OSINT tools grouped by category. Best starting point to discover new tools.
https://osintframework.com

---

#### Photon
Fast web crawler — extracts emails, social media, files, subdomains, API keys. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/s0md3v/Photon

---

#### Amass
Subdomain enumeration and infrastructure mapping. OWASP project, passive DNS sources. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/owasp-amass/amass

---

#### Subfinder
Fast subdomain discovery from passive sources. Free, CLI, commonly used with Amass.
https://github.com/projectdiscovery/subfinder

---

#### Trace Labs OSINT VM
Ready-to-use virtual machine with pre-installed OSINT tools. Based on Kali Linux. Free.
https://www.tracelabs.org/initiatives/osint-vm

---

#### Buscador OSINT VM
Michael Bazzell's OSINT VM with pre-configured tools and VM. Free, maintained by author of "OSINT Techniques" book.
https://inteltechniques.com/tools/

---

#### GHunt
Google account OSINT — reviews, maps, photos, YouTube, calendar from Google ID. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/mxrch/GHunt

---

#### Holehe
Check which services an email is registered on. Similar to Sherlock but for emails. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/megadose/holehe

---

#### ExifTool
Read EXIF metadata from images — GPS, date, device, software. Command line, free.
https://exiftool.org

---

#### Sherlock
Username search across 400+ social platforms. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock

---

#### Maigret
Advanced username search. More platforms than Sherlock, profile analysis, report generation. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/soxoj/maigret

---

#### PhoneInfoga
Phone number OSINT — carrier, location, reputation, social media. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/sundowndev/phoneinfoga

---

#### theHarvester
Email, subdomain, IP enumeration from public sources. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/laramies/theHarvester
