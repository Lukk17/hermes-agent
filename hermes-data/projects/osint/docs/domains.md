# Domains & WHOIS

## Free

#### NASK WHOIS
Official Polish domain registry WHOIS for .pl domains. Shows registrant details, registration date, DNS servers, contact info.
https://whois.nask.pl

---

#### Who.is
Universal WHOIS lookup for all TLDs. Simple interface, supports domain, IP, AS number search.
https://who.is

---

#### DNSdumpster
DNS reconnaissance — subdomain enumeration, MX records, DNS mapping. Shows visual DNS graph of infrastructure.
https://dnsdumpster.com

---

#### crt.sh
Certificate Transparency log search. Find all SSL certificates issued for a domain — reveals subdomains, internal hostnames, issuance dates.
https://crt.sh

---

#### SecurityTrails (Free Tier)
Free tier available — Historical DNS data, WHOIS history, subdomain enumeration, associated domains. Limited but useful.
https://securitytrails.com

---

#### VirusTotal
Free tier — Domain/IP reputation, associated files, URLs, subdomains, passive DNS. One of the best free all-in-one checks.
https://www.virustotal.com

---

#### URLScan
Free — Website scanner. Screenshots, technology detection, associated domains, request logs, linked resources.
https://urlscan.io

---

#### DNSDumpster
Free — Same as above — DNS enumeration, visual map, TXT/MX/NS records.
https://dnsdumpster.com

---

#### ViewDNS.info
Free — Reverse DNS, WHOIS lookup, IP history, DNS propagation checker. All free, no account needed.
https://viewdns.info

---

#### Robtex
Free — DNS lookup, BGP/AS lookup, IP whois, reverse DNS. Good for infrastructure research.
https://www.robtex.com

---

#### DNSlytics
Free tier — DNS lookup, reverse DNS, IP geolocation, ASN information. Limited free queries.
https://dnslytics.com

---

## Paid

#### SecurityTrails (Pro)
~$100/month — Full historical DNS, WHOIS history, subdomain enumeration, associated domain discovery. Essential for deep infra recon.
https://securitytrails.com

---

#### WhoisXML API
~$$100-500/month — Comprehensive WHOIS database, historical WHOIS, DNS data. Best for automated bulk lookups. Multiple tier pricing.
https://www.whoisxmlapi.com

---

#### DomainTools (Pro)
~$100+/month — Historical WHOIS, reverse WHOIS, DNS monitoring, domain reputation scores. Gold standard for domain OSINT.
https://whois.domaintools.com

---

#### Shodan
Free tier (limited), paid from $49/month — Internet-connected device scanner. Port enumeration, service detection, SSL cert data, geolocation. Best for infrastructure footprinting.
https://www.shodan.io

---

#### Censys
Free tier, paid from $50/month — Internet scan data. SSL certificates, hosts, services, vulnerabilities. Alternative to Shodan.
https://search.censys.io

---

#### PassiveTotal (RiskIQ)
~$200+/month — Passive DNS, WHOIS history, malware Sandbox. Used by security teams for threat intel.
https://community.riskiq.com

---

#### WhoisDS
~$50+/month — WHOIS database search, domain registration history, expiry monitoring.
https://whoisds.com
