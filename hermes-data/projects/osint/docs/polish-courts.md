# Polish Courts OSINT

## Free

#### Portal Orzeczeń Sądów Powszechnych
Nationwide searchable judgments — commercial, civil, labor, criminal cases. Covers all Polish common courts.
https://orzeczenia.ms.gov.pl/search

---

#### e-Sąd — Portal Sądu Rejonowego
Electronic case search for selected district courts. Some courts have online case tracking.
https://esqd.pl (varies by court)

---

#### Portal Rejestrów Sądowych (PRS)
Ministry of Justice portal — KRS filings, online registry documents, company status checks.
https://prs.ms.gov.pl

---

#### eKRS — Wyszukiwarka KRS
Official KRS online search. Full company excerpts with management, shareholders, capital history.
https://ekrs.ms.gov.pl/web/wyszukiwarka-krs/strona-glowna/index.html

---

#### CEIDG — Wyszukiwarka
Central Business Activity Register. Sole proprietorship search by name or NIP.
https://aplikacja.ceidg.gov.pl/ceidg/ceidg.public.ui/Search.aspx

---

#### Monitor Sądowy i Gospodarczy (MSiG)
KRS entries, bankruptcy announcements, creditor notices, liquidation notices. Daily publications.
https://www.imsig.pl

---

#### Elektroniczne Księgi Wieczyste (eKW)
Land and mortgage registers. Property owners, encumbrances, mortgages. Requires property deed number.
https://ekw.ms.gov.pl/eukw_ogol/menu.do

---

#### Portal Spółek — S24
Electronic company registration system. New company filings, amendments, liquidations.
https://s24-ekstern.ankw.edu.pl

---

#### Sąd Rejonowy w Lublinie
Example local court portal — each district court has its own portal with local judgments.
https://orzeczenia.lublin.sr.gov.pl

---

#### Sąd Okręgowy w Warszawie
Warsaw District Court rulings — commercial chamber, large cases, appeals.
https://orzeczenia.warszawa.so.gov.pl

---

#### Sąd Apelacyjny w Krakowie
Appeal court rulings — Lesser Poland region, important commercial decisions.
https://orzeczenia.krakow.sa.gov.pl

---

#### KIO — Krajowa Izba Odwoławcza
National Appeals Chamber — public procurement disputes. Company names, tender references, decisions.
https://www.uzp.gov.pl/kio

---

#### UZP — Urząd Zamówień Publicznych
Public Procurement Office — tender regulations, announcements, blacklists of dishonest contractors.
https://uzp.gov.pl

---

#### BIG InfoMonitor
Debt register — judgments related to payment arrears, bad debtors.
https://www.big.pl

---

#### KRD — Krajowy Rejestr Długów
National Debt Register — individual and business debtors, amounts, creditors.
https://krd.pl

---

#### Centralna Informacja KRS
KRS Central Information — company status, registered documents, annual reports.
https://www.krs.com.pl

---

#### Sąd Najwyższy — Orzeczenia
Supreme Court rulings — final appeals, legal interpretations, important precedent cases.
https://www.sn.pl

---

#### NSA — Naczelny Sąd Administracyjny
Supreme Administrative Court — tax disputes, public administration appeals.
https://orzeczenia.nsa.gov.pl

---

#### WSA — Wojewódzki Sąd Administracyjny
Voivodeship Administrative Courts — regional administrative disputes.
https://www.nsa.gov.pl

---

#### Monitor Monitorów
Publication platform for official announcements — bankruptcies, liquidations, changes to companies.
https://monitor-mp.pl

---
