# Geolocation & Mapping OSINT

## Free

#### Google Earth Pro
Satellite imagery, Street View, distance measurement, historical imagery. Free download. Best overall satellite view tool.
https://earth.google.com

---

#### Sentinel Hub (Free Tier)
Copernicus/Sentinel satellite imagery. Historical imagery, change detection over time. Free tier with limited requests.
https://www.sentinel-hub.com

---

#### Overpass Turbo (OpenStreetMap)
Advanced queries on OpenStreetMap data — buildings, roads, POI, infrastructure. Very powerful for area reconnaissance.
https://overpass-turbo.eu

---

#### Mapillary
Crowdsourced street-level photos. Often more recent than Google Street View. Good for area verification.
https://www.mapillary.com

---

#### Google Maps
Satellite, Street View, reviews, photos, posting dates. Useful for physical location verification.
https://maps.google.com

---

#### Bing Maps
Alternative satellite and street imagery. Sometimes has different or older coverage than Google.
https://www.bing.com/maps

---

#### OpenStreetMap
Full OpenStreetMap data — buildings, roads, addresses, facilities, land use. Free, editable.
https://www.openstreetmap.org

---

#### Yandex Maps
Russian mapping service. Often has better coverage in Russia, Central Asia, Eastern Europe than Western alternatives.
https://yandex.com/maps

---

#### ExifTool
Read GPS metadata from photos. Extract location where image was taken. Free, CLI.
https://exiftool.org

---

#### FotoForensics
Free — Error Level Analysis (ELA) on images. Can help detect manipulated or copied images.
https://fotoforensics.com

---

#### TinEye
Reverse image search — find where else on the internet an image appeared, which may reveal original location.
https://tineye.com

---

#### Wigle.net
Free — WiFi network mapping. Shows wireless network locations worldwide. Upload wardriving data or search networks.
https://wigle.net

---

#### Flightradar24
Free tier — Live aircraft tracking, flight paths, historical flight data. Good for tracking target movement.
https://www.flightradar24.com

---

#### MarineTraffic
Free tier — Ship tracking, vessel positions, port arrivals. AIS data for maritime OSINT.
https://www.marinetraffic.com

---

#### Zoom Earth
Free — Near-real-time satellite imagery, weather data, fire detection. Historical imagery available.
https://zoom.earth

---

#### Wikimapia
User-contributed geographic encyclopedia. Detailed place descriptions, sometimes more info than OSM.
https://wikimapia.org

---

## Paid

#### PimEyes
From ~€25/month — Facial recognition search across internet. Upload face photo, find other images of same person. Good for location inference from photos.
https://pimeyes.com

---

#### Sentinel Hub (EO Browser)
From ~€30/month — Full Sentinel imagery access, historical analysis, change detection, multiple sensors.
https://www.sentinel-hub.com

---

#### Planet Labs
Pricing on request — Daily satellite imagery of entire Earth. High resolution, frequent revisit rates. Enterprise pricing.
https://www.planet.com

---

#### Google Earth Pro (Historical Imagery)
Historical imagery in Google Earth Pro is free but limited. For extensive historical analysis consider Sentinel Hub.
https://earth.google.com

---

#### Maxar
Pricing on request — Very high-resolution commercial satellite imagery. Best available resolution for commercial use.
https://www.maxar.com

---

#### Mapbox
From ~$50/month — Custom maps, geocoding, routing, satellite imagery. API-based.
https://www.mapbox.com
