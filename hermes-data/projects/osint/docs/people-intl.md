# People Search — International

## Free

#### Pipl
One of the most comprehensive people search engines. Combines data from social media, public records, deep web. Best for cross-referencing identities across multiple sources.
https://pipl.com

---

#### FastPeopleSearch
Free people search aggregating phone numbers, addresses, emails, relatives. Good coverage for US and some international.
https://www.fastpeoplesearch.com

---

#### TruePeopleSearch
Free lookup by name, phone, address, or email. Clean interface, decent US coverage.
https://www.truepeoplesearch.com

---

#### That's Them
People search — emails, IP addresses, phone numbers. Mainly US data with some international.
https://thatsthem.com

---

#### 411.com
Reverse phone lookup and people search. US-centric but often returns current/residential numbers.
https://www.411.com

---

#### Zabasearch
Free people search engine. Search by name, phone, address. Often returns household member data.
https://www.zabasearch.com

---

#### PeekYou
People search aggregating social media, blogs, news mentions. Good for finding online aliases.
https://www.peekyou.com

---

#### FamilyTreeNow
Free family relationship search. Shows relatives, associates, current/previous addresses. US-focused.
https://www.familytreenow.com

---

#### Spytox
Free people search — phone, email, address lookup. Decent accuracy for US data.
https://spytox.com

---

#### USSearch
Free tier available. Phone lookup, address history, relatives. US-centric.
https://www.ussearch.com

---

#### Webmii
Searches for a person's presence across the web — social profiles, articles, mentions. International.
https://webmii.com

---

#### Google
Sometimes the simplest approach. Use advanced operators: `site:linkedin.com/in "name"`, `intitle:"name"`, `"name" @email.com`
https://www.google.com

---

## Paid

https://www.beenverified.com

---

https://www.instantcheckmate.com

#### Spokeo
~$14.95/month — Aggregates data from social networks, public records, lifestyle databases. International coverage, good for email/phone searches.
https://www.spokeo.com
