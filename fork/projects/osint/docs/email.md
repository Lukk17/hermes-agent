# Email OSINT

## Free

#### HaveIBeenPwned
Check if email appeared in data breaches. Shows breach names, dates, types of data exposed.
https://haveibeenpwned.com

---

#### EPIEOS
Email OSINT — Google ID finder, profile photo, linked accounts, calendar entries from Google account.
https://epieos.com

---

#### Holehe
Check which online services an email is registered on. CLI tool, fast, open source.
https://github.com/megadose/holehe

---

#### GHunt
Google account OSINT — reviews, maps, photos, YouTube, calendar from Google email address.
https://github.com/mxrch/GHunt

---

#### Hunter.io (Free Tier)
Email pattern finder for domains — find company email format (e.g., firstname.lastname@company.com). 25 searches/month free.
https://hunter.io

---

#### Google
Advanced operators for email search: `"email@domain.com" site:linkedin.com`, `"email@domain.com" filetype:pdf`, `"email@domain.com" intitle:"resume"`
https://www.google.com

---

#### BreachDirectory
Check if email in known data breaches. Free, shows partial password hash.
https://breachdirectory.org

---

#### Snov.io
Free tier — Email finder and verifier. Domain email lookup, email validation. 50 credits/month free.
https://snov.io

---

#### EmailFinder
Free tier — Email discovery from domain names. 20 free searches.
https://emailfinder.com
