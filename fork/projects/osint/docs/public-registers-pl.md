# Public Registers — Poland

## Free

#### eKRS — KRS Online Search
Official Polish company registry. Full excerpts — PESEL numbers of management, shareholders, entry history, capital. Best starting point for any Polish company.
https://ekrs.ms.gov.pl/web/wyszukiwarka-krs/strona-glowna/index.html

---

#### CEIDG — Centralna Ewidencja i Informacja o Działalności Gospodarczej
Register of sole proprietorships. NIP, REGON, PKD codes, business status, address, proxies. Search by name or NIP.
https://aplikacja.ceidg.gov.pl/ceidg/ceidg.public.ui/Search.aspx

---

#### Financial Documents Repository (Repozytorium Dokumentów Finansowych)
Official financial statements — balance sheets, income statements, management reports, auditor opinions. Search by KRS number.
https://ekrs.ms.gov.pl/rdf/pd/search_df

---

#### REGON — GUS Internet Database
National business register. Company registration data — NIP, REGON, PKD codes, address, phone, email, ownership form.
https://wyszukiwarkaregon.stat.gov.pl/appBIR/index.aspx

---

#### VAT White List (Biała Lista VAT)
Official Polish VAT payer registry. VAT status, registered bank accounts submitted to tax office, registration date. Critical for due diligence.
https://www.podatki.gov.pl/wykaz-podatnikow-vat-wyszukiwarka

---

#### Electronic Land and Mortgage Registers (Elektroniczne Księgi Wieczyste)
Property ownership records — owners, mortgages, encumbrances, easements. Requires property deed number (numer KW).
https://ekw.ms.gov.pl/eukw_ogol/menu.do

---

#### Geoportal
Cadastral map — plot numbers, administrative units. Use to establish property deed number for EKW lookup.
https://mapy.geoportal.gov.pl/imap/Imgp_2.html

---

#### Public Registers Portal (Portal Rejestrów Sądowych)
New Ministry of Justice portal — KRS filings, online registry document viewer.
https://prs.ms.gov.pl

---

#### Court Rulings Portal — Common Courts (Orzeczenia Sądów Powszechnych)
Searchable judgments and decisions — commercial, civil, labor cases. Nationwide coverage.
https://orzeczenia.ms.gov.pl/search

---

#### SADF — Public Aid Data (SUDOP)
How much public aid a company received — amounts, programs, dates, granting institutions.
https://sudop.uokik.gov.pl/home

---

#### KIO — National Appeals Chamber
Appeals in public procurement — useful if company participated in tenders.
https://www.uzp.gov.pl/kio

---

#### Public Procurement Gateway (e-Zamówienia)
Public tenders — bids, results, contracts, contractors. Search by company name or NIP.
https://ezamowienia.gov.pl

---

#### Court Rulings — District Court in Garwolin
Local court rulings — labor, commercial, civil cases from Garwolin region. Useful for small local businesses.
https://orzeczenia.garwolin.sr.gov.pl

---

#### Court Rulings — District Court in Warsaw
Warsaw District Court rulings — commercial chamber, appeals. Good for larger Warsaw companies.
https://orzeczenia.warszawa.so.gov.pl

---

#### KRD — National Debt Register
Check if person or company is a debtor — amounts, creditors, due dates.
https://krd.pl

---

#### BIG InfoMonitor
BIK debt register — payment arrears for businesses and individuals.
https://www.big.pl

---

#### ERIF BIG
Third major debt register — check and register debtors.
https://erif.pl

---

#### TERYT — Common National Address Register
Administrative division of Poland — localities, streets, postal codes. Use to validate addresses.
https://eteryt.stat.gov.pl

---

#### GIIF — General Inspector of Financial Information
Ultimate beneficial owners of companies. Required for AML/compliance checks.
https://www.giif.gov.pl

---

#### KNF — Polish Financial Supervision Authority
Register of financial institutions, advisors, pension fund entities.
https://www.knf.gov.pl

---

#### RCL — Government Legislation Centre
Legislative projects, company consortium information from law drafts.
https://legislacja.rcl.gov.pl

---

## Paid

#### iMSiG — Monitor Sądowy i Gospodarczy
KRS entries, bankruptcy announcements, creditor notices, liquidation announcements. ~60 PLN/month.
https://www.imsig.pl

---

#### Rejestr.io
Company relationship maps, KRS data, financial statements, public aid, industries. ~100 PLN/month.
https://rejestr.io

---

#### Aleo.com
KRS data, management, capital connections, financial statements, VAT bank accounts, reviews. ~50 PLN/month.
https://aleo.com/pl/firmy

---

#### TransparentData.pl
Company reports, credit scoring, connections, KRS monitoring. B2B pricing.
https://transparentdata.pl

---

#### KRS-online
Alternative KRS search with change history, connections, financial data. ~60 PLN/month.
https://www.krs-online.com.pl

---

#### InfoVeriti
Commercial reports from KRS — finances, management, connections, credibility rating. B2B pricing.
https://www.infoveriti.pl
