# Business Search — International

## Free

#### OpenCorporates
The largest open database of companies in the world. Covers 140+ jurisdictions including Poland. Search by company name, officer, address, industry. Shows filing history, officers, registered address.
https://opencorporates.com

---

#### Crunchbase
Company funding data, founders, investors, acquisitions. Free tier shows basic info. Excellent for startup/tech research.
https://www.crunchbase.com

---

#### Corporation Wiki
Maps relationships between people and companies. Shows corporate connections, job histories, organizational charts.
https://www.corporationwiki.com

---

#### Google
Advanced operators for company research: `site:linkedin.com/company "companyname"`, `site:bloomberg.com "companyname"`, `"companyname" filetype:pdf`
https://www.google.com

---

#### Wayback Machine
Historical versions of company websites — old contact info, team pages, press releases, job postings.
https://web.archive.org

---

#### Google Maps
Business reviews, photos, posting dates, owner responses. Good for physical location verification.
https://maps.google.com

---

#### OpenStreetMap
Crowdsourced map data — business locations, facilities, infrastructure. Good for geolocation.
https://www.openstreetmap.org

---

#### GitHub
If company has public repositories — code, commit history, contributor names, READMEs with company info.
https://github.com

---

## Paid

#### Dun & Bradstreet (Bisnode)
~$100-500/month — Global business credit reports, DUNS numbers, credit scores, bankruptcy data, payment history. Gold standard for B2B due diligence. Has Polish coverage via Bisnode.
https://www.dnb.com

---

#### PitchBook
~$200+/month — Venture capital and private equity data. Funding rounds, investors, M&A, exit data. Strong for startup/tech analysis. Expensive but comprehensive.
https://pitchbook.com

---

#### LinkedIn Sales Navigator
~$80/month — Advanced company and people search on LinkedIn. Shows employee counts, job changes, company growth signals.
https://www.linkedin.com/sales/solutions/sales-navigator

---

#### Bloomberg Terminal
~$25,000+/year — Professional-grade financial data. Company financials, executives, M&A, market data. Overkill for most OSINT but unmatched in depth.
https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/

---

#### ZoomInfo
~$15,000+/year — B2B contact database. Employee lists, company hierarchies, direct contact info. Enterprise pricing.
https://www.zoominfo.com

---

#### Clearbit
~$200+/month — Company data API. Technographics, employee counts, funding, location. Good for enrichment.
https://clearbit.com

---

#### FullContact
~$50-200/month — Company and person data. Email → name, company, social profiles.
https://www.fullcontact.com

---

#### Hunter.io
Free tier (25 searches/month), paid from $49/month — Domain email pattern finder. Find email patterns for companies, verify addresses.
https://hunter.io
