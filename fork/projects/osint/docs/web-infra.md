# Web Infrastructure Analysis

## Free

#### Shodan
Internet scanner — open ports, servers, cameras, IoT, SSL certificates. Search by IP, port, country, product. Essential for infrastructure recon.
https://www.shodan.io

---

#### Censys
Internet scan data — hosts, certificates, services, ports. Alternative to Shodan with different coverage.
https://search.censys.io

---

#### BuiltWith
What technologies a website uses — CMS, analytics, hosting, frameworks. Free tier shows basic tech stack.
https://builtwith.com

---

#### Wappalyzer
Technology detection on websites — CMS, server, framework, CDN. Browser extension and web tool available.
https://www.wappalyzer.com

---

#### SecurityTrails (Free Tier)
Historical DNS data, subdomain enumeration, WHOIS history. Free tier limited but useful.
https://securitytrails.com

---

#### DNSDumpster
DNS reconnaissance — subdomains, MX, NS records, visual DNS map. Free.
https://dnsdumpster.com

---

#### crt.sh
Certificate Transparency log search. Find all SSL certs issued for a domain — reveals subdomains, internal hostnames, CAs.
https://crt.sh

---

#### VirusTotal
Domain/IP reputation, associated files, URLs, subdomains, passive DNS. Free tier very capable.
https://www.virustotal.com

---

#### URLScan
Website scanning and analysis — screenshots, resources, technologies, associations. Free, no account needed.
https://urlscan.io

---

#### Wayback Machine
Historical website versions — old contact info, team pages, organizational structures, offers. Essential for historical research.
https://web.archive.org

---

#### Hunter.io (Free Tier)
Email patterns in domain — find company email format (e.g., first.last@company.com). 25 searches/month free.
https://hunter.io

---

#### DomainTools Whois (Free View)
Basic WHOIS lookup — registrar, dates, nameservers. Limited historical data.
https://whois.domaintools.com

---

#### Who.is
Simple WHOIS lookup for all TLDs. No account needed.
https://who.is

---

#### Google
Advanced operators: `site:example.com`, `intitle:"keyword"`, `inurl:"keyword"`, `related:example.com`
https://www.google.com

---

#### DNSDumpster
DNS enumeration, MX/NS/TXT records, visual infrastructure map. Free.
https://dnsdumpster.com

---

#### Amass
Passive subdomain enumeration via OWASP project. Integrates with Shodan, Crtsh, passive DNS. Free, CLI.
https://github.com/owasp-amass/amass

---

## Paid

#### SecurityTrails (Pro)
From ~$100/month — Full historical DNS, WHOIS history, subdomain enumeration, associated domains. Essential for deep infra recon.
https://securitytrails.com

---

#### WhoisXML API
From ~$100/month — Comprehensive WHOIS database, historical WHOIS, DNS data. Best for automated bulk lookups.
https://www.whoisxmlapi.com

---

#### DomainTools (Pro)
From ~$100/month — Historical WHOIS, reverse WHOIS, DNS monitoring, domain reputation. Gold standard for domain OSINT.
https://whois.domaintools.com

---

#### Shodan (Paid)
From $49/month — Enhanced queries, export, alerts, net scanner access. API access included.
https://www.shodan.io

---

#### Censys (Paid)
From $50/month — Full scan data access, API, historical data, export capabilities.
https://search.censys.io

---

#### PassiveTotal (RiskIQ)
From ~$200/month — Passive DNS, WHOIS history, malware sandbox. Professional threat intelligence tool.
https://community.riskiq.com

---

#### GreyNoise
From ~$100/month — "Internet noise" — identifies if an IP is a bot, scanner, or benign traffic. Useful to filter scanner traffic.
https://www.greynoise.io

---

#### FOCA
Free (open source) — Document metadata extraction. Scans documents on websites for metadata leaks (author, software, paths).
https://github.com/ElevenPaths/FOCA

---

#### ZoomEye
Free tier, paid from ~$30/month — Chinese internet scanner. Similar to Shodan/Censys. Good for devices, webcams, network equipment.
https://www.zoomeye.org
