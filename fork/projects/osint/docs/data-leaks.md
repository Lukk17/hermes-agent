# Data Leaks & Breach Databases

## Free

#### HaveIBeenPwned
Check if email appeared in known data breaches. Shows breach names, dates, types of data exposed. Most well-known free breach checker.
https://haveibeenpwned.com

---

#### BreachDirectory
Check if email in breaches — also shows first few characters of leaked passwords. Free.
https://breachdirectory.org

---

#### IntelX
Search across leaks, paste sites, dark web by email, domain, IP, PESEL, name. Powerful free tier.
https://intelx.io

---

#### LeakCheck
Free tier available — Check if email/username in known leaks. Shows breach names and data types.
https://leakcheck.io

---

#### Snusbase
Free tier limited — Email, username, password hash search in breach databases.
https://snusbase.com

---

#### LeakPeek
Free tier — Breach lookup by email or domain. Lower cost alternative.
https://leakpeek.com

---

#### GhostProject
Free — Search emails in breach data. Shows password if found.
https://ghostproject.fr

---

#### DeHashed
Free tier very limited — Comprehensive breach database search. Paid from $5/month.
https://dehashed.com

---

#### Scylla
Free tier — Aggregate breach search, database of leaked credentials.
https://scylla.sh

---

#### Hunter.io (Free Tier)
Email pattern finder for domains. Not breach data but useful for email OSINT. 25 searches/month free.
https://hunter.io

---

#### Google
Search pastebin/paste sites for leaked data: `site:pastebin.com "email@domain.com"`, `site:ghostbin.co "target"`
https://www.google.com

---
