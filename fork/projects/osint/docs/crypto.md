# Cryptocurrency OSINT

## Free

#### Blockchain.com Explorer
Free — BTC, ETH, BCH, LTC blockchain explorer. Wallet balances, transaction history, public addresses.
https://www.blockchain.com/explorer

---

#### Etherscan
Free — Ethereum blockchain explorer. Smart contract interactions, token transfers, gas prices, ETH wallet tracking.
https://etherscan.io

---

#### Blockstream
Free — Bitcoin explorer with additional features. Bitcoin blockchain data, Lightning Network info.
https://blockstream.info

---

#### Bitcoin Who's Who
Free — Bitcoin address lookup. Known scams, owner information submitted by users, address tags.
https://bitcoinwhoswho.com

---

#### Whale Alert
Free — Real-time blockchain transactions above certain thresholds. Twitter @whale_alert, website.
https://whale-alert.io

---

#### WalletExplorer
Free — Bitcoin wallet clustering and tagging. Groups addresses into wallets, shows trading relationships.
https://www.walletexplorer.com

---

#### Amberdata
Free tier — Blockchain data platform. NFT tracking, DeFi data, on-chain analytics.
https://amberdata.io

---

#### DeBank
Free — DeFi portfolio tracker. Shows wallets, token holdings, DeFi interactions across multiple chains.
https://debank.com

---

#### Etherscan (BTC-ETH Converter)
Free — Track Bitcoin-to-Ethereum cross-chain transactions via wrapping (WBTC).
https://etherscan.io

---

#### Blockchain Analysis (Self)
Free — Manual blockchain analysis using public explorers. Cross-reference addresses, trace transaction flows.
https://www.blockchain.com/explorer
