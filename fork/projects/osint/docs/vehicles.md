# Vehicles & Transportation

## Free

#### CEPiK — Centralna Ewidencja Pojazdów (Historia Pojazdu)
Free official Polish vehicle history. Requires VIN or plate + registration number. Shows: registration history, mileage readings, ownership records, accident damage, recalls. Polish vehicles only.
https://historiapojazdu.gov.pl

---

#### Geoportal
Map-based property and land database. Use to identify properties, then cross-reference with vehicle registration data. Polish only.
https://mapy.geoportal.gov.pl

---

#### Google Maps / Street View
Satellite and street-level imagery. Useful for verifying vehicle presence at addresses, identifying property types, parking lots, garages.
https://maps.google.com

---

#### OpenStreetMap
Crowdsourced map data — gas stations, parking lots, car dealerships, repair shops. Good for area reconnaissance.
https://www.openstreetmap.org

---

#### Bing Maps
Alternative to Google Maps. Sometimes has different or older imagery coverage.
https://www.bing.com/maps

---

#### Tapatalk
Community-driven vehicle info boards and club databases. Sometimes useful for vehicle identification by VIN or plate.
https://www.tapatalk.com

---

## Paid

#### AutoCheck (Experian)
~$25-40 per report — Vehicle history report. Accident history, title checks, odometer fraud, salvage records. US-focused but covers some imported vehicles.
https://www.autocheck.com

---

#### CARFAX
~$39.99 per report — Most well-known vehicle history service. Damage reports, ownership counts, service records, recall data. US and Canada primarily.
https://www.carfax.com

---

#### InstaDrive
Price varies — VIN lookup and vehicle analytics. Registration data, accident history, market value estimates.
https://instadrive.com

---

#### VinAudit
~$9.99 per report — Affordable VIN check. Title history, accident indicators, odometer records. US-focused.
https://www.vinaudit.com

---

#### VehicleHistory
~$20-30 per report — Comprehensive vehicle reports. Title, accident, salvage, auction records. US vehicles.
https://www.vehiclehistory.com
