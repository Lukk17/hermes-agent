# Social Media — OSINT

## Free

#### Sherlock
Open-source username search across 400+ social platforms. CLI tool, fast, good for initial sweep.
https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock

---

#### Maigret
Advanced Sherlock fork. More platforms, profile analysis, report generation. Better than Sherlock for comprehensive checks.
https://github.com/soxoj/maigret

---

#### Social Searcher
Real-time social media post search. Free tier limited. Searches across multiple networks simultaneously.
https://www.social-searcher.com

---

#### Twitter/X Advanced Search
Advanced tweet search — date filters, geolocation, engagement metrics, user mentions. Free, most powerful for open-source research.
https://twitter.com/search

---

#### Instagram
Public profiles, hashtags, locations. Use web version or third-party tools. No native advanced search.
https://www.instagram.com

---

#### Facebook
Public groups, pages, events. Limited without account but web scraping possible.
https://www.facebook.com

---

#### LinkedIn
Professional network — job histories, company connections, education. Public profiles available without login.
https://www.linkedin.com

---

#### TikTok
Video search, trending content, user profiles. Web version available.
https://www.tiktok.com

---

#### Reddit
Community discussions, user profiles, post history. Good for finding opinions, connections, activity patterns.
https://www.reddit.com

---

#### Pinterest
Image-based discovery. User boards, pins, profile info. Sometimes reveals interests and locations.
https://www.pinterest.com

---

#### VK (VKontakte)
Russian social network. Public profiles, groups, photos. Useful for Eastern European targets.
https://vk.com

---

#### Odnoklassniki (OK)
Russian social network. Similar to Facebook, older demographic in Russia.
https://ok.ru

---

#### Flickr
Photo sharing platform with EXIF data. Some users still active. Good for historical image searches.
https://www.flickr.com

---

#### Tumblr
Blogging platform. Archives, posts, likes. Sometimes reveals personal interests and connections.
https://www.tumblr.com

---

## Paid

#### Social Links
Comprehensive social media OSINT platform. Analytics, profile enrichment, cross-platform identity linking. Price varies by tier.
https://sociallinks.io

---

#### BuzzGuru
Social media analytics and influencer search. YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter stats. Pricing varies.
https://buzzguru.com

---

#### Twitonomy
Twitter analytics — follower graphs, tweet history, engagement metrics. ~$5-15/month.
https://twitonomy.com

---

#### SocialBlade
Social media stats — YouTube, Twitch, Twitter, Instagram growth metrics. Free tier available, pro from $15/month.
https://socialblade.com

---

#### TweetDeck
Twitter/X monitoring and advanced search. Free with account. Scheduling, lists, multi-column search.
https://pro.twitter.com

---

#### PhantomBuster
Automated social media scraping. LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter, Facebook data extraction. $99+/month.
https://phantombuster.com

---

#### apricot
LinkedIn data extraction and outreach. High-quality professional data. Pricing on request.
https://apricot.io

---

#### intelligence2day
Social media and news monitoring for OSINT. Professional tool, pricing on request.
https://intelligence2day.com

---

#### Nuelink
Social media analytics and scheduling. Post history, follower growth. ~$25/month.
https://nuelink.com

---

#### Socialinsider
Social media analytics platform. Cross-platform reporting, engagement metrics. ~$99/month.
https://www.socialinsider.io
