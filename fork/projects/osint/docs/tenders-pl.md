# Public Procurement — Poland

## Free

#### e-Zamówienia (BZP)
Official Polish public procurement gateway. Tenders, bids, results, contracts, contractors. Search by company name or NIP.
https://ezamowienia.gov.pl

---

#### Platforma Zakupowa
Modern alternative procurement platform. Similar coverage to e-Zamówienia, different interface.
https://platforma-zakupowa.pl

---

#### KIO — Krajowa Izba Odwoławcza
National Appeals Chamber. Appeals in public procurement — useful if company participated in tenders and had disputes.
https://www.uzp.gov.pl/kio

---

#### TED — Tenders Electronic Daily
EU procurement database. European-wide tender notices, results, contract awards. Search by country, company name, CPV codes.
https://ted.europa.eu

---

#### Public Procurement Office (Urząd Zamówień Publicznych)
Polish procurement regulator. Rules, announcements, guidance.
https://uzp.gov.pl

---

## Paid

#### e-Zamówienia (Premium)
Some enhanced features may require account. Full search history, saved searches, export. Free to register.
https://ezamowienia.gov.pl

---

#### Platforma Zakupowa (Pro)
Enhanced features for subscribers. Advanced filtering, alerts, API access.
https://platforma-zakupowa.pl

---

#### Przetargi.pl
Polish tender aggregator. ~100 PLN/year for premium — enhanced search, alerts, tender monitoring.
https://przetargi.pl

---

#### Tenders Insight
International tender intelligence. Broader coverage than just Poland. Pricing on request.
https://tendersInsight.com
