# Data Aggregators

## Free

#### OpenCorporates
Largest open company database in the world. 140+ jurisdictions including Poland. Company name, officer, address, industry search. Filing history, officers, registered address.
https://opencorporates.com

---

#### Google
Free search often beats paid databases for surface-level info. Use advanced operators: `site:linkedin.com/company "name"`, `site:bloomberg.com "name"`, `"name" filetype:pdf`
https://www.google.com

---

#### Wayback Machine
Historical data, old website versions, removed content, past employee lists. Free.
https://web.archive.org

---

#### Crunchbase (Free Tier)
Company funding, founders, investors, acquisitions. Free tier gives basic info. Good for startup/tech research.
https://www.crunchbase.com

---

## Paid

#### Bisnode / Dun & Bradstreet
From ~$100/month — Global business credit reports, DUNS numbers, credit scores, payment history, bankruptcy data. Gold standard for B2B due diligence. Polish coverage via Bisnode.
https://www.dnb.com

---

#### Rejestr.io
From ~100 PLN/month — Relationship maps (people and capital), KRS data, financial statements, finances, industries, public aid. Best Polish aggregator.
https://rejestr.io

---

#### iMSiG — Monitor Sądowy i Gospodarczy
From ~60 PLN/month — KRS entries, bankruptcy announcements, creditor notices, liquidation announcements.
https://www.imsig.pl

---

#### Aleo.com
From ~50 PLN/month — KRS data, management, capital connections, financial statements, VAT bank accounts, reviews.
https://aleo.com/pl/firmy

---

#### TransparentData.pl
B2B pricing — Company reports, scoring, connections, KRS change monitoring.
https://transparentdata.pl

---

#### KRS-online
From ~60 PLN/month — Alternative KRS search with change history, connections, financial data.
https://www.krs-online.com.pl

---

#### InfoVeriti
B2B pricing — Commercial reports from KRS — finances, management, connections, credibility rating.
https://www.infoveriti.pl

---

#### PitchBook
From ~$200/month — Venture capital and private equity data. Funding rounds, investors, M&A, exits. Expensive but best for startup/tech analysis.
https://pitchbook.com

---

#### ZoomInfo
From ~$15,000+/year — B2B contact database. Employee lists, company hierarchies, direct contact info. Enterprise pricing.
https://www.zoominfo.com

---

#### Corporation Wiki
Pricing varies — Maps relationships between people and companies. Corporate connections, job histories, organizational charts.
https://www.corporationwiki.com

---

#### Clearbit
From ~$200/month — Company data API. Technographics, employee counts, funding, location. Good for data enrichment.
https://clearbit.com

---

#### FullContact
From ~$50/month — Company and person data. Email → name, company, social profiles.
https://www.fullcontact.com
